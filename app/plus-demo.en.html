Method and sources

The overall score is an illustrative model of assessable areas. Data coverage and missing values are excluded. It does not rank you against other investors.

Diversification considers underlying exposure, position weights and identifiable overlap. It does not contain a complete look-through of every fund constituent. Financial base considers recorded monthly headroom, payments, your chosen interest threshold and available reserves. Regularity uses up to twelve months of recorded purchases. Costs use fully recorded order fees only.

There is no point deduction for holding no gold or crypto. Price comparisons after a sale prove neither panic nor a wrong decision. The stress scenario is an assumption, not a forecast.

Frequently asked questions

Do you pay less tax in Portugal than in Germany?

Portugal uses up to 48% for income and depends on income type for capital gains (reference rates), while Germany sits at up to 45% and 26.375%. Allowances, local rules and double tax treaties still decide your personal bill.

When does tax liability in Germany actually end?

Merely travelling abroad does not automatically end tax residence. A permanent home, vital interests or exit tax rules may still apply. Check a tax adviser before moving.

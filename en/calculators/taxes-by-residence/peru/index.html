Before you move to Peru

  • Clarify where you will be tax resident and whether your previous country still treats you as resident.
  • Check how Peru taxes foreign income, and whether only money brought into the country is taxed.
  • Look for a double tax treaty between Peru and the countries your income comes from.
  • Keep a record of your days per country, flights and rental contracts.
  • Get tax advice for your situation before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

How high is income tax in Peru?

The top income tax rate in Peru is up to 30%. Country reference for general orientation. Income, region, asset type and special rules can change the personal rate.

How is capital income taxed in Peru?

The reference value for capital income in Peru is 5%. Allowances and the type of income (gains, dividends, interest) matter.

When do I become tax resident in Peru?

That depends on the rules of Peru, often on a permanent home, the centre of vital interests or around 183 days of presence. Check the official sources and get advice for your situation.

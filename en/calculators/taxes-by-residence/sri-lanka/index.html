Before you move to Sri Lanka

  • Clarify where you will be tax resident and whether your previous country still treats you as resident.
  • Check how Sri Lanka taxes foreign income, and whether only money brought into the country is taxed.
  • Look for a double tax treaty between Sri Lanka and the countries your income comes from.
  • Keep a record of your days per country, flights and rental contracts.
  • Get tax advice for your situation before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

How high is income tax in Sri Lanka?

The top income tax rate in Sri Lanka is up to 36%. The individual investment-asset gains rate increased to 15% on 3 June 2026; the older 10% rate is not generally current.

How is capital income taxed in Sri Lanka?

The reference value for capital income in Sri Lanka is 15%. Allowances and the type of income (gains, dividends, interest) matter.

When do I become tax resident in Sri Lanka?

That depends on the rules of Sri Lanka, often on a permanent home, the centre of vital interests or around 183 days of presence. Check the official sources and get advice for your situation.

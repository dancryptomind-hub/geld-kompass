FAQ

What is a Dividend King?

A company that has raised its dividend for at least 50 consecutive years. In this list: Altria, PepsiCo, Procter & Gamble, AbbVie, Coca-Cola, Johnson & Johnson, Walmart. It shows a very stable business but does not guarantee future increases.

What is a Dividend Aristocrat?

A company in the US S&P 500 index with at least 25 consecutive years of dividend increases, for example Realty Income, McDonald’s or ExxonMobil.

Which stocks pay monthly?

Realty Income. Three quarterly payers paying in different months also give you money every month.

How is the yield calculated?

Sum of the last 12 months of payouts per share, converted to euro, divided by the current price. Future dividends may fall or be cancelled.

How do I get dividends every month?

Combine holdings with different payment months, e.g. quarterly payers in Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct, Feb/May/Aug/Nov and Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec. The Geld-Kompass shows the result for your portfolio.

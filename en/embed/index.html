Frequently asked questions

Which calculators are included?

The 11 calculators listed in the configurator: Savings plan, emergency fund, freedom days, debt-free, snowball/avalanche, 4% rule, credit card payoff, inflation, consumer or owner, currency converter and taxes by residence. The full Money Compass app and dividend calendar are not included in the embed licence.

What data and calculation methods are used?

Calculators provide model estimates with their respective inputs, assumptions or sources. The inflation calculator uses annual-average Eurostat HICP data, the currency converter displays the exchange-rate data timestamp, and taxes by residence lists country references and sources. Check the displayed data date and coverage. No guarantee of real-time data or individual tax outcomes.

What about privacy?

See the privacy notice. Compliance also depends on your website, configuration and applicable jurisdiction. Check required notices and consent before publishing.

How are licences and additional services agreed?

Prices are in EUR. Applicable taxes, billing, term, cancellation and licence scope are confirmed in the quote before ordering. Custom calculators, self-hosting and additional customisation require a separate agreement. Existing contracts are unaffected.

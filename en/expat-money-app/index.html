Days per country and Schengen 90/180

Enter when you arrived where. The Compass counts your days per country and year, shows your Schengen status over the last 180 days and warns you when you approach the common 183-day mark. As guidance, not tax advice.

Every currency, one overview

Book in baht, lari, dong or dollars. The Compass converts with daily rates into your main currency so your monthly report, portfolio and freedom days add up.

What a month costs in which country

For each country you see your average monthly spending. So you know how many freedom days life costs you where, and can choose your next stop on purpose.

Works without a local bank account

No bank access needed. Enter rough numbers or import statements as files, for example from Wise or Revolut. The Compass then spots subscriptions and fixed costs by itself.

Sending money between countries? Check first in the currency converter with live rates.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app tell me where I am tax resident?

No. It counts your days as guidance. 183 days is a common rule but not universal. Where you are tax resident is something to clarify with a tax adviser.

Which currencies work?

50+ currencies with daily rates, plus bitcoin. You choose your main currency.

Do I need a bank account or an account?

No. Money Compass Pro runs in the browser and as an app on your phone, without an account and without bank access.

Does it cost anything?

Freedom days, budget, debt plan and currency converter are free. Nomad mode is part of Plus, the upcoming subscription. Until launch you can try it for free in the preview.
